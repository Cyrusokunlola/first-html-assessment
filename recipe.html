How to Make Pancakes

This recipe teaches you how to make delicious fluffy Pancakes, that are tasty and sweet.

  • Milk
  • Eggs
  • Flour
  • Oil
  • Baking powder
  • Salt
  • Butter
  1. Mix dry ingredients: Flour, Sugar, Baking powder, Salt
  2. Mix wet ingredients: Butter, Eggs, Milk
  3. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and stir until just combined do not overmix, or the pancakes will turn out dense.
  4. Rest the batter for 5 mins
  5. Use a non-stick pan
  6. Put the pan on fire and pour the batter
  7. Flip after the batter has bubbles on the surface
  8. Cook until golden brown and serve while warm
Sauté
To cook food quickly in a small amount of fat over relatively high heat, stirring or tossing frequently.
Simmer
To cook a liquid at just below boiling point, so small bubbles form gently rather than a rolling boil.
Fold
To gently combine a light, airy mixture (like whipped egg whites) into a denser one, using a scooping motion to avoid deflating it
